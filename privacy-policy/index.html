1.Introduction

IFX Marketing ("IFX Marketing", "we", "us", "our") is a digital marketing agency providing performance marketing and lead generation services to fintech, financial services, stock market, forex, and trading businesses. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or interact with advertising and forms we operate.

By using our website or submitting information to us, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not submit personal information to us.

2.Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

This may include your name, email address, phone number, business details, service inquiries, and any other information you include in forms or messages.

Information collected automatically

Where applicable, we or our service providers may collect your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, cookies, and website interaction data.

Advertising and lead data

We may receive information that people submit through Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Lead Ads, website forms, WhatsApp communications, and other advertising channels we use.

3.How We Use Personal Information

  • Responding to inquiries and service requests.
  • Managing leads and business communications.
  • Providing fintech digital marketing and lead-generation services.
  • Measuring advertising performance and improving campaigns.
  • Preventing fraud, maintaining security, and meeting legal obligations.

4.Legal Bases for Processing

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on one or more of these lawful bases:

  • Consent – for example, when you submit a form, agree to be contacted, or accept non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests – such as operating our business, securing our systems, and improving our services, balanced against your rights.
  • Contractual necessity – where processing is needed to provide services you request or to take steps before entering a contract.
  • Legal obligation – where we must process data to comply with applicable law.

5.Meta Advertising and Tracking Technologies

Advertising and measurement technologies such as the Meta Pixel, Conversions API, cookies, analytics, and remarketing may be used to measure advertising performance and reach relevant audiences. We use these technologies only where they are actually implemented on our website or campaigns.

When you submit a Meta Lead Ad form, the information you enter may be provided to us. Merely viewing or clicking an advertisement does not mean IFX Marketing automatically receives your personal details from Meta. Meta processes data under its own policies; you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

Site owner: confirm which of these tools are live before publishing and remove any that are not in use.

6.Sharing of Personal Information

Where applicable, we may share information with hosting providers, CRM providers, analytics providers, advertising platforms, communication tools, and other service providers who support our operations. These parties may only use the information for the services they provide to us or as their own terms allow.

We may disclose information to legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety. We share information only for legitimate business purposes and as legally required.

7.Sale of Personal Information

Some laws define "sale" or "sharing" broadly, and may include certain advertising and analytics technologies that share data with third parties for targeted advertising. We distinguish between (a) selling personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration and (b) sharing information with advertising platforms for targeted advertising or measurement, where applicable.

Where required by law, you may opt out of sale or sharing by contacting us at the email below.

Site owner: if you have confirmed that you never sell personal information, you may add that statement here.

8.International Data Transfers

We and our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, which may have different data protection laws. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as contractual protections or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

9.Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

10.Cookies and Tracking

Where used, cookies generally fall into these categories:

  • Essential – needed for the site to work and stay secure.
  • Analytics – help us understand how the site is used.
  • Advertising – support ad measurement and remarketing.

Where the law requires consent for non-essential cookies, we will request it. You can also control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may be affected.

11.Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to help protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12.Privacy Rights

EU/EEA (GDPR)

Eligible individuals may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of their data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent, as well as to complain to their local supervisory authority.

California

Where applicable, California residents may have the right to know and access, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing, without discrimination for exercising these rights.

India (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules in force, individuals may have rights such as access to information, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, and nomination, subject to the law.

These rights depend on applicable law and circumstances, and some requests may be limited by legal exceptions. To make a request, email us at connect.ifx@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first.

13.Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Any information we handle is treated according to applicable age-related laws. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

14.Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website and advertising may link to third-party sites and services, including Meta and WhatsApp. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing information.

15.Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected on this page, and continued use of our services after an update means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.
